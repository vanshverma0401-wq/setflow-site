SetFlow

SetFlow is a strength-training log for iPhone. Log your sets in kilograms or pounds, follow your progress, and see which muscle groups you have trained recently.

Your workouts stay on your iPhone. SetFlow has no account and no ads, and it does not send your data to any server.

SetFlow is a fitness app. It does not give medical advice.
