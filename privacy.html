Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

In short: SetFlow keeps your data on your iPhone. We do not collect it, we do not sell it, and we do not share it. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What SetFlow stores, and where

Everything you enter in SetFlow is stored on your iPhone: your workouts, sets and weights, body measurements, progress photos, food and water logs, schedules and settings. Most of it is kept in the app's private storage; a few profile values, such as your height and weight, are kept in the iPhone's secure Keychain. SetFlow does not upload any of it to us or to any other server, and it does not sync it to iCloud.

Like any app, SetFlow's data can be included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer) if you use them. Those backups are handled by Apple and by you, not by SetFlow.

Apple Health

If you allow it, SetFlow reads these types from Apple Health to show your training and recovery context: sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, active energy, steps, time in daylight, body weight, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, body temperature and cycle data. You choose which of them to allow. If you allow it, SetFlow can also save mindful minutes from its breathing and recovery sessions to Apple Health.

Health data is used only on your iPhone to provide SetFlow's features. It is never sent to us, never shared with anyone, never used for advertising or marketing, and never stored in iCloud by SetFlow. You can change or remove SetFlow's access at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

Other permissions

  • Camera: when you take a meal photo in the food scanner. The photo stays on your iPhone.
  • Photo library: to show the progress photos you choose, and to save a workout share image when you ask SetFlow to save it.
  • Calendar: if you schedule a workout, SetFlow can add it as an event to your calendar. After that, the event belongs to your Calendar app and is synced by your calendar account (for example iCloud or Google), like any event you add yourself. SetFlow does not read your other events.
  • Microphone: only when you choose to use the audio monitor; audio is used on your iPhone and SetFlow does not send it anywhere.
  • Face ID: to lock the app. Face ID is handled by iOS; SetFlow never receives your face data.
  • Notifications: for reminders such as rest timers, sent by your iPhone itself.

Every permission is optional. SetFlow works without the ones you decline, with fewer features.

Exports you choose to share

If you export your workouts (for example as a CSV or JSON file), the file goes only where you send it, using the iOS share sheet.

Deleting your data

Deleting SetFlow from your iPhone deletes the data in its private storage. Values kept in the Keychain, such as your height and weight, can stay on the iPhone after the app is deleted; they never leave the device, and you can ask us how to clear them. Entries SetFlow saved to Apple Health stay in Health, and calendar events it added stay in your calendar, where you can delete them.

Children

SetFlow is not directed to children under 13.

Not medical advice

SetFlow is a fitness app. Its numbers and suggestions are for training only and are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds optional analytics or subscriptions, we will update this page and its date before that version is released.
