  • LICENSE-NOTICE.md (full credits and the list of changes)

Credits

  • "Z-Anatomy - The libre 3D atlas of anatomy - CC-BY-SA 4.0", from Z-Anatomy (Gauthier Kervyn, Marcin Zielinski, Lluis Vinent). Used for the muscle regions and the muscle definition baked into the skin.
  • "BodyParts3D - The Database Center for Life Science - CC-BY-SA 2.1 Japan", the original model that Z-Anatomy adapted.
  • The human body surface comes from MakeHuman assets, released under CC0 1.0.

What we changed

We kept only the muscular system, simplified it, split the body surface into regions that match SetFlow's muscle groups, baked the muscle definition into the skin, and re-oriented the figure. The notice file lists every change.

Anyone who shares this model, or a model made from it, must give the credits above and use the same licence. The SetFlow app's own code is not covered by this licence.
