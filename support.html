Common questions

How do I switch between kilograms and pounds?
Open Settings in SetFlow and choose your weight unit. Your past workouts are converted for display; nothing you logged is changed.

Where is my data?
On your iPhone only. SetFlow has no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

Can I move my data to a new iPhone?
Restoring your iPhone from a backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) brings SetFlow's data with it. You can also export your workouts from SetFlow as a file.

How do I delete my data?
Delete the SetFlow app. Entries SetFlow saved to Apple Health stay in the Health app, where you can delete them.

What do I need?
An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
